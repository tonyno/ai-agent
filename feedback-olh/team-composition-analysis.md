# Team Composition — Person Profiles (Intake & RCM)

> **Purpose:** Aggregate all leader feedback into one profile per person ahead of the team scale-down (next 60 days), so DMT can decide **where to assign each person** and **who wants to work with whom**.
>
> **How to read a profile:** each card lists the person's role, the **score from every rater**, an aggregated view of **strengths** and **watch-outs** (verbatim feedback condensed), and a **placement note**.
>
> **Scale:** raters used roughly **1 (no preference) → 5 (must-have for my next team)**. Wording varied ("want again" / "rank" / "preference"); treat scores as directional.

- **RCM:** Nastya (Product Lead), Muhammad (Tech Lead)
